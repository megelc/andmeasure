## 5. Measurements and accuracy

AndMeasure calculates distances and areas from the points you place on
the map and from your phone's location. The results are **estimates**.
Their accuracy depends on how precisely the points are placed, the map
imagery, the map's scale and projection, and your phone's GPS signal,
which can be off by several metres or more. Units such as bigha, katha
or guntha differ between regions; the app uses the values described in
its help.

The app is **not a surveying instrument**. Do not use its results as the
only basis for legal, financial, construction or safety decisions, such
as property boundaries, land sales, official documents, building work or
navigation. For those, use a licensed surveyor or an official source. PDF
reports show what you measured in the app and are not certified
documents.

Circles mode alerts you when your phone's location crosses a circle's
edge. Because location can be inaccurate or delayed, do not rely on
alerts for safety, for example near water, cliffs, roads or restricted
areas.
