Questions

How many guntha are in an acre?

40. One guntha is 1,089 square feet, or 121 square yards (gaj).

How many cents are in an acre?

100. One cent is 435.6 square feet. In Bangladesh the same unit is called a decimal (shotangsho).

How many marla are in a kanal, and kanal in an acre?

20 marla make a kanal, and 8 kanal make an acre, so an acre is 160 marla. This converter uses the revenue marla of 272.25 ft². Some housing schemes use a smaller marla of 225 ft², so check which one a plot is sold in.

How many square feet are in a bigha?

It depends on the state. In West Bengal and Bangladesh a bigha is 14,400 ft² (20 katha of 720 ft²), and that is the bigha used here. In other states a bigha can be anywhere up to about 27,000 ft², so use the local value.
