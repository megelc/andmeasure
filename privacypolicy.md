# AndMeasure privacy policy

*Last updated 28 October 2026.*

AndMeasure is made by Mikkel Christensen, Denmark. This policy explains what
happens to your data when you use the app.

**What the app does not do.** AndMeasure currently has no user accounts,
no advertising and no analytics, and the developer receives no data from
the app. If that changes, this policy will say so first.

**Your location** is used on your phone only: to show where you are, to
measure as you walk, and to tell you when you enter or leave a circle. It
is not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can deny the location
permission; the app then works without your position.

**Your measurements** are stored on your phone. If Android backup is on
for your Google account, Android includes them in that backup; you can
switch this off in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app deletes
them from the phone.

**Sharing and exports.** When you share a measurement (CSV, KML, picture or
PDF report), it goes only to the app you choose to share it with.

**Google services.** The app uses Google services, which receive some data
directly under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy):

- *Google Maps* loads the map for the area you view and collects technical
  data such as your device model, IP address, crash reports and how the map
  is used.
- *Place search* sends the text you search for to Google to find the place.
  Nothing is sent unless you search.
- *Purchases* of AndMeasure Pro are handled by Google Play. The app does
  not see your payment details. Google gives the developer the order
  details shown in Play Console (such as the order number, country and
  price), which are used only to handle the purchase, refunds and support.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children.

**Your rights.** The developer holds no personal data about you from the
app. If you write for support, your email is used only to answer you and
is deleted when no longer needed. For questions or requests under the
GDPR, contact: **andmeasure@gmail.com**.

**Changes.** Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new
date.
